## 1. Getting Started

### What Can Employees Do?

As an employee, your main responsibilities in the system are:

- **Create support tickets** when you have IT problems or need help
- **View your own tickets** to check their status and progress
- **Track your equipment** to see what devices are assigned to you
- **Update your profile** with personal information
- **View dashboard overview** to see quick system statistics

### What Can't Employees Do?

Employees cannot:
- Create user accounts (only administrators can do this)
- Assign tickets to technicians
- Modify other employees' tickets or data
- Delete tickets
- View other employees' information
- Generate detailed reports

## 2. Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the IT Help Desk System URL provided by your IT department
2. You will see the login page with two fields: Username and Password
3. Enter the username provided by your IT administrator
4. Enter the initial password provided to you
5. Click the **Login** button
6. On your first login, you will be asked to change your password to a new one
7. Enter a new password that is at least 6 characters long
8. Confirm the new password by typing it again
9. Click **Change Password** to complete
10. You will now be logged into the system

### Subsequent Logins

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Login**
5. You will be redirected to the dashboard

### Forgot Your Password?

1. Click the **Forgot Password?** link on the login page
2. Enter your email address or username
3. Click **Reset Password**
4. You will receive an email with a password reset link
5. Click the link in the email
6. Enter your new password (minimum 6 characters)
7. Confirm the password
8. Click **Save New Password**
9. You can now log in with your new password

### Tips for Logging In

- Always use your official company username provided by IT
- Passwords are case sensitive, so be careful with uppercase and lowercase letters
- Do not share your password with anyone including IT staff
- Always log out when you finish using the system, especially on shared computers
- Clear your browser cache if you experience login issues

## 3. Creating Support Tickets

### When to Create a Ticket

Create a support ticket when you have any of these issues:

- Computer or laptop problems (slow, not starting, crashes)
- Software issues (application won't open, errors, bugs)
- Network or internet problems (no connection, slow connection)
- Printer or hardware problems (not printing, device not working)
- Password resets or access issues
- Equipment assignment or tracking
- Any other IT-related problem or question

### How to Create a Ticket

1. Click on **Tickets** in the left navigation menu
2. Look for the **Create Ticket** or **New Ticket** button (usually blue, at the top right)
3. Click it to open the ticket creation form
4. Fill in the required fields:

#### Ticket Title
- Briefly describe the problem in a short title
- Good example: "Laptop won't start up"
- Bad example: "Problem"
- Keep it to 5-10 words

#### Description
- Provide detailed information about the issue
- Include what you were doing when the problem occurred
- Describe any error messages you see
- Explain how the problem affects your work
- Example: "My Dell laptop will not power on. I tried holding the power button for 30 seconds but nothing happens. The power indicator light does not turn on either."

#### Category
- Select the type of problem from the dropdown menu:
  - **Hardware** - Computer, printer, monitor, keyboard, etc.
  - **Software** - Applications, programs, errors
  - **Network** - Internet, WiFi, connection issues
  - **Other** - General questions, access issues, other

#### Priority
- Select how urgent the problem is:
  - **Low** - Can wait a few days, does not affect work (example: printer not available, can use alternative)
  - **Medium** - Should be fixed soon, some impact on work (example: application running slowly)
  - **High** - Urgent, significantly affects work (example: cannot access important files)
  - **Urgent** - Critical, stops all work (example: computer completely down, cannot log in)

5. Review the information you entered to make sure it's correct
6. Click **Submit** or **Create Ticket** to submit your ticket
7. You will see a success message with your ticket ID
8. Write down your ticket ID for reference
9. You will receive an email confirmation with your ticket number

### Tips for Creating Tickets

- Be as detailed as possible so the technician understands the problem
- Include your location or office number
- Mention what devices you are using (laptop, desktop, etc.)
- Include any error messages word for word
- Tell us what you have already tried to fix it
- Select the correct category for faster assignment
- Use clear language, avoid technical jargon if possible
- Submit high priority tickets as soon as problems occur
- One issue per ticket - don't combine multiple problems

## 4. Viewing Your Tickets

### Finding Your Tickets

1. Click on **Tickets** in the left navigation menu
2. You will see a list of all your tickets
3. The list shows ticket ID, title, status, priority, and creation date
4. Your tickets are filtered to show only those you created

### Ticket Statuses

Each ticket has a status showing where it is in the process:

- **Open** - Your ticket was just created and not yet assigned to a technician
- **In Progress** - A technician is working on your ticket right now
- **Resolved** - The technician has fixed the problem
- **Closed** - The ticket is complete and archived

### Viewing Ticket Details

1. Find the ticket in your list
2. Click on the ticket to open the detail view
3. The detail page shows:
   - Full ticket information you entered
   - Current status
   - Assigned technician name
   - Creation date and time
   - Last updated date and time
   - Comments and updates from the technician
   - Ticket priority

### Sorting and Filtering

1. Click on any column header (Title, Status, Priority, Date) to sort by that column
2. Click again to reverse the sort order
3. Use the search bar to find tickets by title or ID
4. Filter by status to see Open, In Progress, Resolved, or Closed tickets
5. Filter by priority to see High, Medium, Low, or Urgent tickets

### Exporting Ticket List

1. At the top of the ticket list, look for **Export** or **Download** button
2. Click it to download your tickets as a file
3. The file will be saved to your Downloads folder
4. You can open it in Excel or view it in your browser

## 5. Tracking Ticket Progress

### How to Check Ticket Status

1. Go to **Tickets** → **My Tickets**
2. Look at the Status column to see current status
3. The color of the status indicates progress:
   - Red = Open (waiting to be assigned)
   - Orange = In Progress (being worked on)
   - Green = Resolved (fixed, waiting for closure)
   - Gray = Closed (complete)

### How to View Technician Updates

1. Click on your ticket to open the detail page
2. Scroll down to the **Comments** or **Updates** section
3. You will see all messages from the assigned technician
4. Read the latest update to understand what has been done
5. The timestamp shows when each update was posted

### Estimated Resolution Time

1. On the ticket detail page, look for **Estimated Completion** or **Target Date**
2. This shows when the technician expects to finish
3. If it's taking longer than expected, you can add a comment asking for an update

### Adding Comments to Your Ticket

1. Open your ticket
2. Scroll down to the comment box at the bottom
3. Type your message or question
4. Click **Add Comment** or **Send**
5. The technician will see your comment and can respond
6. Use comments to provide additional information or ask for updates

### Getting Notifications

1. Go to **Settings** or **Preferences**
2. Look for **Notifications** options
3. Enable ticket notifications if available
4. You will get alerts when:
   - Your ticket is assigned to a technician
   - The status changes
   - A technician adds a comment
5. Notifications may come via email or in-app alerts

## 6. Viewing Your Equipment

### Accessing Your Equipment

1. Click on **Assets** in the left navigation menu
2. You will see a list of all equipment
3. Filter or search to find equipment assigned to you

### Understanding Equipment Information

Each piece of equipment shows:

- **Asset Name** - What the equipment is called (Dell Laptop, HP Printer)
- **Asset Code** - Unique identifier printed on the equipment
- **Category** - Type of equipment (Computer, Printer, Monitor, etc.)
- **Status** - Available, Assigned, Maintenance, or Disposed
- **Condition** - Good, Fair, or Poor
- **Assigned To** - Name of who is using it (should be your name)
- **Location** - Where the equipment is located

### Finding Your Assigned Equipment

1. Go to **Assets**
2. In the filter or search, select your name or search for your username
3. Only your assigned equipment will show
4. Note down the asset codes for your equipment

### Checking Equipment Condition

1. On the asset list or detail page, look for the **Condition** field
2. **Good** - Equipment working normally
3. **Fair** - Equipment working but may need maintenance
4. **Poor** - Equipment needs repair soon

### Reporting Equipment Issues

If your equipment is damaged or not working:

1. Go to **Tickets** and create a new support ticket
2. Set category to **Hardware**
3. Describe the problem with the equipment
4. Include the asset code of the equipment
5. Set priority to **High** if it affects your work
6. A technician will create a repair log for that equipment

## 7. Managing Your Profile

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click on your **Profile** link (usually in the top right corner)
2. Or go to **Settings** → **Account** or **My Profile**
3. You will see your account information:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Full name
   - Department
   - Role
   - Account status (Active or Inactive)

### Updating Your Information

1. Open your profile
2. Look for an **Edit** or **Edit Profile** button
3. You can typically update:
   - Phone number
   - Office location
   - Department
   - Other personal information
4. Do NOT change your username or role - contact administrator for these
5. Click **Save** to save your changes

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to your Profile
2. Look for **Change Password** or **Update Password** option
3. Enter your current password (for verification)
4. Enter your new password (at least 6 characters)
5. Confirm the new password by typing it again
6. Click **Save** or **Change Password**
7. Your password is now updated
8. Use your new password on your next login

### Password Requirements

Your password must:
- Be at least 6 characters long
- Be different from your previous passwords
- Not be the same as your username
- Be kept confidential

### Tips for Password Security

- Change your password every 3-6 months
- Don't use dictionary words or names
- Use a mix of letters and numbers if possible
- Don't write your password down
- Don't share your password with coworkers
- Use different passwords for different accounts

5. Priority: **High**
6. Submit the ticket
7. Wait for technician assignment
8. Check your ticket regularly for updates

### Task: Reporting a Slow Application

1. Go to **Tickets** → **Create Ticket**
2. Title: "Excel application running very slowly"
3. Description: "Microsoft Excel takes 10+ seconds to open files and is very slow when scrolling through data. This is affecting my productivity."
4. Category: **Software**
5. Priority: **Medium**
6. Submit the ticket
7. The technician will investigate

1. Go to **Tickets** → **Create Ticket**

## 10. Troubleshooting

### I Forgot My Username

**Problem**: I can't remember my username for login

**Solution**:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click **Forgot Password?** link
3. Enter your full name or email address
4. Follow the instructions sent to your email
5. You may see your username in the email
6. Or contact your IT administrator with your name and they can tell you

### I'm Locked Out of My Account

**Problem**: I tried logging in too many times with wrong password and now I can't log in

**Solution**:
1. Wait 15-30 minutes before trying again
2. Or click **Forgot Password?** to reset your password
3. If still locked out, contact your IT administrator
4. They can unlock your account and reset your password

### I Can't Create a Ticket

**Problem**: When I try to create a ticket, I get an error or the button doesn't work

**Solution**:
1. Refresh your web browser
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Close and reopen the browser
4. Try using a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
5. Check your internet connection
6. If still not working, contact IT support with details of what happened

### My Ticket Is Taking Too Long to Resolve

**Problem**: My ticket has been in progress for days and no updates

**Solution**:
1. Add a comment to your ticket asking for an update
2. Check if the priority level was set correctly (urgent problems get faster service)
3. Provide more details or clarification if the description was vague
4. Contact your supervisor or IT manager if it's urgent
5. For truly critical problems, call the IT support hotline

### I Can't See My Equipment in the Assets List

**Problem**: When I search for my equipment, it doesn't show up

**Solution**:
1. Check if you are filtering by the correct name or search term
2. Try searching by your full name instead of just first or last name
3. Check the asset condition - if it says "Disposed" it won't show in available equipment
4. It might be assigned to someone else - check with them
5. Contact IT if you think you should have equipment but it's not showing

### I'm Getting Error Messages When I Try Actions

**Problem**: I see error messages like "Permission Denied" or "Access Forbidden"

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you are logged in with your correct account
2. As an employee, some actions are restricted - you may not have permission
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Log out and log back in
5. Contact your administrator if you think you should have access to something
6. Employees have limited permissions by design

### The Dashboard Isn't Showing All My Tickets

**Problem**: My Tickets count shows fewer tickets than I created

**Solution**:
1. Close tickets are not always shown by default
2. Check if there is a filter excluding closed tickets
3. Remove any active filters to see all tickets
4. Refresh the page to get latest data
5. Old closed tickets might be hidden - adjust the date range

### I Forgot My Changes to My Profile

**Problem**: I tried to edit my profile but my changes were not saved

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you clicked the **Save** button
2. Some fields cannot be edited (like username and role) - these are locked
3. Try editing again and click **Save** clearly
4. Look for a success message confirming changes were saved
5. Refresh the page to see if changes took effect
6. Contact IT if changes still won't save

### I Can't Upload Files or Attachments

**Problem**: I want to attach a file to my ticket but can't find an upload option

**Solution**:
1. Some systems don't allow file attachments for security reasons
2. You can describe the issue in the ticket description instead
3. You can provide file contents in the ticket comments
4. Ask the technician if they need a file - they may request it separately
5. Use cloud storage like email or shared drive to send files to IT staff

### My Browser Won't Display the Page Correctly

**Problem**: The page looks broken, buttons are in wrong places, text is cut off

**Solution**:
1. Clear your browser cache - press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Close the browser completely and reopen it
3. Try zooming out (press Ctrl and minus key)
4. Use a different browser to see if it works
5. Try on a different computer if available
6. Contact IT support if problem persists

### I Received an Email About Account Verification

**Problem**: My account shows as not verified, what do I do?

**Solution**:
1. This is normal for new accounts
2. Only an administrator can verify accounts
3. Wait 24-48 hours for an administrator to verify your account
4. Do not try to verify yourself
5. Once verified, you will get a confirmation email
6. Contact your administrator if not verified after 48 hours

### I Think My Password Was Compromised

**Problem**: I think someone knows my password or my account is being used by someone else

**Solution**:
1. Change your password immediately
2. Go to **Profile** → **Change Password**
3. Enter a new password that is very different from the old one
4. Contact your IT administrator to report the security issue
5. Check your ticket activity - make sure all tickets are yours
6. Administrators may need to reset your account

### The System is Running Slow

**Problem**: The system is very slow, pages take a long time to load

**Solution**:
1. Check your internet connection speed
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Close other browser tabs or applications to free up computer memory
4. Restart your browser
5. Try accessing during a different time (the system might be busy)
6. Contact IT support - there might be a server issue affecting everyone
