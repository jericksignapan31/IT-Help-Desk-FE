## 1. Getting Started

### What Can Supervisors Do?

As a supervisor, your primary responsibilities in the system are:

- **Create support tickets** for your own issues and on behalf of team members
- **View all tickets created by your team members** in your department
- **Approve or reject tickets** submitted by your team
- **Add supervisor comments** and feedback to tickets
- **Escalate urgent issues** to IT management
- **Track team equipment** and asset assignments
- **Manage team member accounts** (password resets, updates)
- **Access reports and analytics** about your team's IT activity
- **View performance metrics** for your department
- **Export data** for reporting and analysis

### What Can't Supervisors Do?

Supervisors cannot:
- Delete tickets or permanently remove data
- Assign tickets to technicians (only IT staff can do this)
- Modify other departments' data or tickets
- Create new user accounts (only administrators can do this)
- Change system settings or configurations
- Access other departments' information
- Modify their own permissions or role

## 2. Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the IT Help Desk System URL provided by your IT department
2. You will see the login page with two fields: Username and Password
3. Enter the username provided by your IT administrator
4. Enter the initial password provided to you
5. Click the **Login** button
6. On your first login, you will be asked to change your password to a new one
7. Enter a new password that is at least 6 characters long
8. Confirm the new password by typing it again
9. Click **Change Password** to complete
10. You will now be logged into the system
11. You will see the Supervisor Dashboard

### Subsequent Logins

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Login**
5. You will be redirected to your supervisor dashboard

### Forgot Your Password?

1. Click the **Forgot Password?** link on the login page
2. Enter your email address or username
3. Click **Reset Password**
4. You will receive an email with a password reset link
5. Click the link in the email
6. Enter your new password (minimum 6 characters)
7. Confirm the password
8. Click **Save New Password**
9. You can now log in with your new password

### Tips for Logging In

- Always use your official supervisor username provided by IT
- Passwords are case sensitive - be careful with uppercase and lowercase letters
- Do not share your password with anyone, including IT staff
- Always log out when you finish, especially on shared computers
- Clear your browser cache if you experience login issues
- Do not store your password in your browser unless on a secure, private computer

## 3. Creating and Managing Your Own Tickets

### When to Create a Ticket

Create a support ticket for:

- Your own computer or equipment problems
- Department-wide issues affecting multiple team members
- Access or permission issues for your team
- Software or hardware needs for your department
- General IT questions or inquiries
- Equipment requests or changes
- Any IT-related problem affecting your responsibilities

### How to Create a Ticket

1. Click on **Tickets** in the left navigation menu
2. Look for the **Create Ticket** or **New Ticket** button (usually blue, at the top right)
3. Click it to open the ticket creation form
4. Fill in the required fields

#### Ticket Title
- Briefly describe the issue in a short, clear title
- Good example: "Department printer needs replacement toner"
- Bad example: "IT Problem"
- Keep it to 5-10 words

#### Description
- Provide detailed information about the issue
- Explain the context and how it affects operations
- For department issues, specify which team members are affected
- Include any attempts you've made to resolve it
- Example: "Our department's main printer is running low on toner and the print quality is degrading. We need to replace the toner cartridge. This is affecting all 12 team members in our department."

#### Category
- Select the type of problem from the dropdown:
  - **Hardware** - Printers, computers, monitors, peripherals
  - **Software** - Applications, licenses, software issues
  - **Network** - Internet, connectivity, access issues
  - **Other** - General requests, questions, other

#### Priority
- Select the urgency level:
  - **Low** - Can wait several days (example: cosmetic issue, minor inconvenience)
  - **Medium** - Should be addressed within 2-3 days (example: department inconvenience)
  - **High** - Urgent, affects operations (example: multiple team members impacted)
  - **Urgent** - Critical, department cannot function (example: all systems down)

#### Assign to Team Member (Optional)
- You can create a ticket on behalf of a team member
- Select your team member's name from the dropdown
- This lets them know you're handling it for them

5. Review the information to ensure accuracy
6. Click **Submit** or **Create Ticket**
7. You will see a success message with the ticket ID
8. Keep the ticket ID for your records
9. You will receive an email confirmation

### Ticket Fields and Options

**Supervisor-Specific Options**:
- **Department** - Automatically populated with your department
- **On Behalf of** - Allows you to create tickets for team members
- **Priority Level** - You may have more priority levels available
- **Attachments** - Some systems allow you to attach files or documents

### Tips for Creating Tickets

- Be as detailed as possible so technicians can resolve issues quickly
- Include the number of team members affected
- Mention your department name
- For equipment issues, include asset codes if available
- For multiple issues, create separate tickets for each one
- Use appropriate priority levels - saves IT time and ensures quick response
- Include error messages exactly as they appear
- Specify the impact on department operations
- Add dates and times when problems occur

## 4. Viewing Your Team's Tickets

### Accessing Team Tickets

1. Click on **Tickets** in the left navigation menu
2. Look for **Team Tickets** or **Department Tickets** option
3. Click it to view all tickets created by your team members
4. You will see a list of all department tickets
5. This includes tickets your team members created and tickets you created for them

### Understanding Team Ticket List

The ticket list shows:

- **Ticket ID** - Unique identifier for each ticket
- **Title** - Brief description of the issue
- **Created By** - Which team member submitted the ticket
- **Status** - Current state (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
- **Priority** - Urgency level
- **Created Date** - When the ticket was submitted
- **Last Updated** - When it was last modified

### Filtering by Team Member

1. Use the filter dropdown to filter by team member name
2. Select one or more team members to see only their tickets
3. This helps you focus on specific team members' issues
4. You can also select "All Team Members" to see everyone

### Sorting and Searching

1. Click on any column header to sort by that field
2. Click again to reverse sort order
3. Use the search bar to find tickets by ID or title
4. Filter by status to see Open, In Progress, Resolved, or Closed
5. Filter by priority to focus on urgent items
6. Use date filters to view tickets from specific time periods

### Ticket Status and Progress

Each ticket has a status:

- **Open** - Ticket submitted, waiting for assignment
- **In Progress** - Technician is actively working on it
- **Resolved** - Issue fixed, waiting for closure
- **Closed** - Ticket complete and archived

Color coding helps identify status at a glance:
- Red = Open (needs attention)
- Orange = In Progress (being worked on)
- Green = Resolved (nearly complete)
- Gray = Closed (complete)

## 5. Approving and Escalating Tickets

### Ticket Approval Workflow

Some tickets may require supervisor approval before technicians work on them. The workflow is:

1. Team member creates a ticket
2. System notifies you for approval
3. You review and approve or reject
4. If approved, ticket goes to IT
5. If rejected, ticket returns to team member

### When to Approve a Ticket

Approve a ticket when:

- The issue is valid and affects department operations
- The request is appropriate for IT support
- The team member should get this help
- The information provided is complete
- The priority level seems correct

### How to Approve Tickets

1. Go to **Tickets** → **Team Tickets** or **Pending Approval**
2. Find the ticket needing approval
3. Click on it to open the detail view
4. Review all the information provided
5. Look for an **Approve** button
6. Click **Approve** to send it to IT technicians
7. Add an optional comment approving the request
8. The team member will be notified

### Rejecting Tickets

If a ticket is not appropriate or needs more information:

1. Open the ticket
2. Click **Reject** or **Request Changes**
3. Add a comment explaining why you're rejecting it
4. Examples: "Please provide more details", "This should be handled through HR", "Try restarting first"
5. The ticket is returned to the team member
6. They can revise and resubmit if needed

### Adding Supervisor Comments

1. Open any team member's ticket
2. Scroll down to the comments section
3. Type your comment or feedback
4. Click **Add Comment** or **Post**
5. Your comment is visible to the technician and team member
6. Use comments to:
   - Provide context about the issue
   - Suggest a workaround while waiting for repair
   - Add priority information
   - Explain the impact on operations
   - Share relevant details

### Escalating Urgent Issues

For critical issues affecting your department:

1. Open the ticket
2. Look for **Escalate** button
3. Click to escalate to IT management
4. Add a comment explaining the urgency
5. Example: "All 15 team members affected. Department cannot operate. Needs immediate attention."
6. Escalated tickets get priority from IT management
7. You will be notified when escalated ticket is addressed

## 6. Managing Team Equipment and Assets

### Viewing Team Equipment

1. Click on **Assets** in the left navigation menu
2. Filter or search to show only your team's equipment
3. You will see all equipment assigned to your team members
4. This includes computers, monitors, printers, and peripherals

### Tracking Asset Assignments

Equipment list shows:

- **Asset Name** - What the equipment is (Dell Laptop, HP Printer)
- **Asset Code** - Unique identifier
- **Category** - Type of equipment
- **Status** - Available, Assigned, Maintenance, or Disposed
- **Condition** - Good, Fair, or Poor
- **Assigned To** - Which team member has it
- **Location** - Where it's physically located
- **Purchase Date** - When acquired
- **Last Maintenance** - When last serviced

### Reporting Equipment Issues

If team equipment needs repair:

1. Click on the equipment to open details
2. Look for **Create Repair Ticket** or **Report Issue** button
3. Or go to **Tickets** and create a new ticket
4. In ticket description, include:
   - Asset code
   - Description of the problem
   - How it affects the team member
   - Urgency level
5. Set priority based on impact
6. Submit ticket
7. Technician will be assigned to repair

### Equipment Maintenance Tracking

1. In asset details, you can see maintenance history
2. This shows all repairs and maintenance performed
3. Use this to plan equipment replacement
4. If equipment is frequently repaired, consider replacement
5. Keep records for budget planning

### Requesting Equipment Changes

To request new equipment or modifications:

1. Go to **Assets**
2. Click **Request New Equipment** or **Equipment Request**
3. Specify equipment type and details
4. Explain the business need
5. Include priority and timeline
6. Submit request
7. IT administrator will review and process

## 7. Viewing Team Reports and Analytics

### Accessing Supervisor Dashboard

1. Click on **Dashboard** in the left navigation menu
2. The supervisor dashboard shows department-specific statistics
3. This is different from the employee dashboard
4. You can see trends and metrics for your team

### Key Metrics and Statistics

The supervisor dashboard displays:

- **Total Team Tickets** - All tickets created by your team
- **Tickets by Status** - Count of Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed
- **Average Resolution Time** - How long tickets take on average

### Ticket Volume Reports

1. Go to **Reports** section
2. Select **Ticket Volume** or **Ticket Statistics**
3. Choose date range (this week, this month, last quarter)
4. View:
   - Total tickets submitted
   - Tickets resolved
   - Tickets pending
   - Tickets by category
5. Use this to understand team IT needs
6. Shows trends over time

### Team Performance Analysis

1. In **Reports**, select **Team Performance**
2. View how your team is using IT support
3. See which team members need more IT training
4. Identify most common issues
5. Compare your department to others (if available)
6. Use this to:
   - Plan training
   - Identify equipment needs
   - Improve processes
   - Set expectations

### Exporting Reports

1. After viewing a report, look for **Export** or **Download** button
2. Choose export format:
   - **PDF** - For printing and sharing
   - **Excel** - For further analysis
   - **CSV** - For importing to other systems
3. Click **Export**
4. File will download to your Downloads folder
5. Use exported reports for:
   - Management reporting

## 8. Managing Team Member Accounts

### Viewing Team Members

1. Click on **Users** or **Team Members** in the menu
2. You will see a list of your department's team members
3. The list shows:
   - Name
   - Username
   - Email
   - Department
   - Role
   - Account Status
   - Last Login

### Updating Team Member Information

1. Click on a team member's name to open their profile
2. Look for **Edit** button
3. You can update:
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Office location
   - Department assignment
   - Contact information
4. You cannot change their username or role
5. Click **Save** to update

### Resetting Team Member Passwords

If a team member forgets their password:

1. Go to **Team Members**
2. Find the team member
3. Click **Reset Password** button
4. System will generate a temporary password
5. You receive the temporary password
6. Provide it to the team member securely (not via email)
7. Team member logs in with temporary password
8. They will be prompted to create a new password
9. Do not share temporary passwords via email
10. Use in-person delivery or secure messaging if possible

### Disabling/Enabling Accounts

To deactivate an account (e.g., employee leaving):

1. Open the team member's profile
2. Look for **Disable Account** or **Deactivate** button
3. This prevents them from logging in
4. Their data is preserved (not deleted)
5. Click **Confirm** to disable
6. You will receive a confirmation

To reactivate:

1. Open the team member's profile
2. Look for **Enable Account** or **Activate** button
3. Click to reactivate
4. They can now log in again

### Managing Permissions

Most supervisors have limited permission management:

1. Some systems allow updating team member permissions
2. You may be able to enable/disable certain features
3. You cannot grant administrator permissions
4. Contact your IT administrator for permission changes
5. Document permission changes for audit purposes

## 10. Common Supervisor Tasks

### Task: Approving a Team Member's Support Request

1. Team member creates a ticket for help
2. You receive notification to approve
3. Go to **Tickets** → **Pending Approval**
4. Click on the ticket to review
5. Read the description and details provided
6. Add a comment if needed: "Approved - team member needs this support"
7. Click **Approve**
8. Team member is notified that you approved
9. Ticket moves to IT technician queue
10. You can follow progress by viewing the ticket

### Task: Responding to Escalated Issues

When an urgent issue arises:

1. A team member or technician may escalate to you
2. Go to **Tickets** → **Escalated** or **Urgent**
3. Review the escalated ticket
4. Add your perspective as supervisor
5. Example comment: "This team member is critical to operations. Department cannot function. Urgent resolution needed."
6. Click **Escalate to Management** if needed
7. Set priority to **Urgent** if not already
8. Follow up directly with IT manager
9. Monitor progress until resolved

### Task: Generating Weekly Team Report

1. Go to **Reports** section
2. Select **Weekly Team Summary**
3. Choose current week
4. The report shows:
   - How many tickets created
   - How many resolved
   - What categories
   - Which team members
   - Open issues
   - Pending actions
5. Click **Export to PDF**
6. File downloads
7. Use for team meetings or management reporting
8. Share with your manager if needed

### Task: Assigning Equipment to New Team Member

When a new person joins your team:

1. Go to **Assets** or **Equipment**
2. Click **Assign Equipment** or **New Assignment**
3. Select the team member (must already have user account)
4. Select equipment from the list
5. Set assignment date
6. Add any notes
7. Include asset codes in notes
8. Click **Assign**
9. Equipment is now listed as assigned to them
10. Create a ticket notifying them of assigned equipment

### Task: Handling Password Reset Requests

Team member forgets password:

1. Team member contacts you or IT
2. Go to **Team Members**
3. Find the team member
4. Click **Reset Password**
5. System generates temporary password
6. Write down the temporary password
7. Call or speak in person to team member
8. Provide password verbally (not in email)
9. Tell them to change it on first login
10. Ask them to confirm they received it and can log in

### Task: Reviewing Team Performance Metrics

Weekly or monthly review:

1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. Review the statistics displayed
3. Look for:
   - Team members with many tickets (may need training)
   - Common issue types (may indicate system problem)
   - Long-pending tickets (may need escalation)
   - Equipment in poor condition (may need replacement)
4. Create an action plan:
   - Do we need equipment upgrades?
   - Do team members need IT training?
   - Are there systemic issues to address?
   - What should be budgeted?
5. Export reports for documentation
6. Share insights with team and management

## 11. Troubleshooting

### I Can't See My Team Member's Ticket

**Problem**: A team member says they created a ticket but it doesn't appear in my team list

**Solution**:
1. Check if the team member is assigned to your department
2. Use search to find the ticket by ID (team member should have ID)
3. Search by ticket title to locate it
4. Check if ticket was assigned to wrong department
5. Verify team member's account is active and assigned to your department
6. Contact IT if ticket is missing - it may not have been created successfully
7. Ask team member to check their email for confirmation

### I Can't Approve Tickets

**Problem**: I don't see an Approve button on tickets or approval doesn't work

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you have supervisor permissions
2. Check if your account is configured for approvals
3. Not all tickets may require supervisor approval
4. Try refreshing the page
5. Check if ticket is already approved or rejected
6. Contact IT administrator to enable approval feature
7. Verify your role is set to Supervisor

### I'm Getting Permission Errors

**Problem**: I get "Access Denied" or "Permission Denied" messages when trying to do tasks

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you're logged in with your supervisor account
2. As a supervisor, you have limited permissions by design
3. You can only manage your own department
4. You cannot access other departments' data
5. You cannot change system settings
6. Contact your IT administrator if you need additional permissions
7. Document what you're trying to do and why you need access

### Report Generation is Failing

**Problem**: When I try to generate a report, I get an error or the report won't load

**Solution**:
1. Try a shorter date range
2. Refresh the page and try again
3. Try exporting in a different format (PDF vs Excel)
4. Check if you have sufficient data for the selected period
5. Clear your browser cache
6. Try a different browser
7. Contact IT support if reports consistently fail
8. Provide details: which report, what error message, when it happens

### The Dashboard Isn't Showing Current Data

**Problem**: The dashboard shows old information, not recent tickets and updates

**Solution**:
1. Click the **Refresh** button on the dashboard
2. Press Ctrl+F5 to hard refresh the page
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Wait a few minutes - data may be updating
5. Check if there's a data refresh schedule (may update hourly)
6. Check if other users are seeing the same issue
7. Contact IT if data is consistently out of date
8. Try accessing at a different time

### I Can't Update Team Member Information

**Problem**: I try to edit a team member's profile but changes won't save

**Solution**:
1. Make sure you clicked the **Save** button
2. Some fields cannot be edited by supervisors (like username, role)
3. Check if there are validation errors (required fields, format)
4. Try editing fewer fields at once
5. Refresh the page and try again
6. Log out and back in
7. Some fields can only be changed by administrators
8. Contact IT for permission changes or account changes

### I Can't Reset a Team Member's Password

**Problem**: The password reset button doesn't work or I can't find it

**Solution**:
1. Make sure the account is active (not disabled)
2. Only active accounts can have passwords reset
3. Check if you have permission to reset passwords
4. The feature may only be available for your department
5. Some systems require admin approval for resets
6. Try clicking directly on the team member's name first
7. Look for "Reset Password" in the Actions menu
8. Contact IT administrator if feature is not available

### Equipment Not Showing in My Team's List

**Problem**: Equipment assigned to my team member doesn't show in the assets list

**Solution**:
1. Check if equipment is properly assigned to the team member
2. Check if the team member is in your department
3. Check if equipment status is "Active" not "Disposed"
4. Try removing filters and viewing all equipment
5. Search by asset code to find specific equipment
6. Search by team member name
7. Equipment may still be in processing
8. Contact IT if equipment is missing after 24 hours

### I'm Locked Out of My Account

**Problem**: I tried logging in too many times with wrong password and can't access my account

**Solution**:
1. Wait 15-30 minutes before trying again
2. Or use the Forgot Password link to reset
3. If locked out for security reasons, contact your IT administrator
4. Administrators can unlock supervisor accounts
5. You may be reset to a temporary password
6. You can then change your password
7. Be more careful with password attempts going forward

### The System is Very Slow

**Problem**: Reports take forever to load, dashboard is sluggish

**Solution**:
1. Try during off-peak hours (early morning, late evening)
2. Close other browser tabs to free up memory
3. Restart your browser
4. Clear your browser cache
5. Try generating smaller date ranges
6. Check your internet speed
7. Try on a different computer if available
8. Contact IT if slowness affects everyone

### I Can't Export Reports

**Problem**: Export button doesn't work or the file won't download

**Solution**:
1. Try a different export format (PDF instead of Excel)
2. Check if your browser allows downloads
3. Check your Downloads folder - file may already be there
4. Disable browser pop-up blockers that might block downloads
5. Try downloading a smaller report
6. Refresh page and try again
7. Try a different browser
8. Contact IT if export feature is not working
